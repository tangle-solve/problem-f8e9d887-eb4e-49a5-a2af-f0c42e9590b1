### ALGO-000110: Vertex Cover below factor two
- **Subfield:** Approximation algorithms and constraint satisfaction
- **Open:** Give a polynomial-time \((2-\varepsilon)\)-approximation for general Vertex Cover for some fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), or establish impossibility from a weaker assumption than UGC.
- **Status:** The elementary factor-two algorithm remains a central benchmark. UGC implies hardness below two, but UGC is unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would settle one of the clearest gaps between known approximation algorithms and unconditional hardness.
- **Key works:** Dinur and Safra (2005), *On the Hardness of Approximating Minimum Vertex Cover*; Khot and Regev (2008), *Vertex Cover Might Be Hard to Approximate to Within 2−ε*.
